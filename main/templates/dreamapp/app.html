How It Works

A Practice, Not a Chatbot

Dream Analytica isn't an AI that invents dream meanings for you. It's a private journal built around Carl Jung's method — you log, the app reflects, and a small daily habit does the rest.

1
Log

Capture pre-sleep context at night, then your dream itself in the morning — mood, stress, and sleep quality included.

2
Insight

See how your dream relates to your waking life — on-device, in plain language, the moment you save.

3
One Lesson

Learn a single Jungian concept at a time — Shadow, Anima/Animus, individuation — no overwhelm.

{# ---------- Step: phased journaling ---------- #}
Night & Morning

Journaling in two phases, by design

Jung's Law of Compensation says dreams often balance a one-sided waking attitude rather than replaying the day. So the journal is split: write what happened before bed, then record the dream after waking — no more than five tags per entry, plus your own personal associations.

Voice memo and speech-to-text are built in for mornings when typing feels like too much.

{# ---------- Step: Dream Health & Compensation Index ---------- #}
On-Device Scoring

Dream Health & Compensation Index

Every entry is scored on-device across seven psyche dimensions — Signal, Inner Weather, Dream Tone, Symbolic Depth, Self-Reflection, Integration, and Continuity — into two headline numbers: your Dream Health and your Compensation Index.

A reflection tool, not a medical or clinical score.
{# ---------- Step: streaks & long-range trend ---------- #}
Continuity

A habit you can watch grow

Streaks keep the practice alive, while a rolling balance chart shows your psyche's trend across a week, month, half-year, or year — so a single hard night is read in context, not in isolation.

{# ---------- Step: recurring symbols ---------- #}
Pattern Intelligence

When an image keeps visiting, follow the thread

Recurring motifs are surfaced automatically — a word, symbol, or theme that keeps appearing across your journal, with every dream it touched laid out in one place.

{# ---------- Step: personal symbol library ---------- #}
Association Before Dictionary

Your Symbol Library, not a universal dictionary

Stop asking what water "means." Every symbol you tag builds a personal library — what it means to you, tracked across every dream it appeared in. Mythic amplification only enters when you have no personal association yet, and it's always labeled as a hypothesis.

{# ---------- Step: journal + export ---------- #}
Your Vault, Your Choice

Export a Jungian brief — never a built-in AI chat

Your full journal stays local. When you're curious, export a structured, ethics-first Jungian report to paste into an AI of your choice — framed as a starting place, not a verdict. Real depth work stays human.
